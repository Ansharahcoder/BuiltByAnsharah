Who Am I?

My name is Ansharah. I am a passionate Full-Stack Web Developer dedicated to designing and building clean, modern, and user-centric digital web solutions.

My core technical focus centers on the MERN Stack (MongoDB, Express.js, React.js, and Node.js). I enjoy translating Figma layout concepts into interactive frontend applications while engineering robust backend RESTful APIs for database management.
